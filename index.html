Our Story

Born from Passion

Polarvista Safaris was founded by Paulette Mureithi out of a deep passion for travel, nature, and the untamed beauty of Africa. Growing up inspired by the breathtaking landscapes, diverse wildlife, and rich cultures of East Africa, she envisioned a company that would not only offer safaris—but create unforgettable life experiences. With determination and a clear vision, Paulette set out to build a brand that connects travelers to the heart of the wild. She believed that a safari should be more than just a journey—it should be a story, a discovery, and a meaningful connection with nature and local communities. Starting with a bold dream and a commitment to excellence, Polarvista Safaris was established to provide personalized, authentic, and immersive travel experiences across Africa’s most iconic destinations. The company focuses on crafting unique adventures that blend comfort, adventure, and cultural richness—ensuring every traveler leaves with memories that last a lifetime. Driven by passion, guided by purpose, and inspired by the wild, Polarvista Safaris continues to grow as a trusted name in the travel industry, turning dreams of exploration into reality.

Wildlife & Safari

The Great Migration & Beyond

Witness 1.5 million wildebeest thunder across the Mara River. Track the Big Five through Amboseli's open savannah with the silhouette of Kilimanjaro on the horizon. Our wildlife journeys are led by naturalists who have spent decades reading the land.

Cultural Heritage

Living Traditions

Spend nights with Maasai elders, sail a hand-carved dhow along the Swahili coast, and wander Lamu's coral-stone streets with a local historian.

Adventure & Trekking

Highland Expeditions

Ascend Mount Kenya through bamboo forest and alpine moorland. Trek the Aberdares in the mist. These routes are for those who prefer to earn their views.
